About the Practice

Specialist care
for lasting smiles

Dr. Mohana's Dental Care is helmed by Dr. D. Mohanalakshmi, a qualified Prosthodontist and Implantologist. The clinic provides a full spectrum of dental services — from preventive check-ups and orthodontics to complex implant surgeries — all under one roof in Adyar, Chennai.

We believe that beautiful dental health is a lifelong journey. Our approach emphasises early prevention, precise diagnosis and treatment plans tailored to each patient's unique needs and goals.

"Every tooth counts — and so does every patient who trusts us with their smile."

— Dr. D. Mohanalakshmi, M.D.S

Root Canal Treatment

Pain-free root canal procedures to save severely infected or damaged teeth, restoring full function and comfort.

02

Crown & Bridge

Custom-crafted dental crowns and bridges to restore and protect teeth, providing natural aesthetics and long-term durability.

03

Orthodontic Braces

Traditional and ceramic orthodontic braces to align teeth and correct bite issues for a confident, balanced smile.

04

Dental Implants

State-of-the-art titanium implants as a permanent, natural-looking replacement for missing teeth — designed to last a lifetime.

05

Invisible Aligners

Discreet, removable aligner trays that gradually straighten teeth without the visibility of traditional metal braces.

06

Cosmetic Dentistry

Smile makeovers using veneers, whitening and bonding — carefully crafted to enhance the natural beauty of your smile.

07

Removable & Fixed Dentures

Comfortable, well-fitted dentures — both removable and implant-supported fixed options — restoring function and appearance.

08

Child Dentistry

Gentle, child-friendly dental care to build positive habits from an early age and keep young smiles healthy and bright.

09

Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Safe, carefully managed surgical and non-surgical wisdom tooth extractions to relieve pain and prevent future complications.

10

Smile Makeover

Full aesthetic transformations combining multiple procedures to completely revamp the look, feel and confidence of your smile.

All General Dental Procedures

Scaling, polishing, fillings, fluoride treatments and routine check-ups — complete general dentistry for the whole family.
